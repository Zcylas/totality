1. OWNERSHIP BOUNDARY — BANKER VS. MERCHANT
================================================================================
RECOVERED — ADOPTED. The Banker and an item merchant are different
roles, not the same NPC type reused.

  BANKER OWNS: opening/upgrading a player account, depositing/
    withdrawing Credits, exchanging physical/dimensional currencies
    into universal Credits, account-tier services, transaction
    history, banking dialogue, bank security responses.

  BANKER DOES NOT OWN: selling Credits to the player (the player does
    NOT buy Credits, full stop), acting as the first ordinary item
    trader, replacing the merchant/shop system.

  MERCHANT OWNS: selling items from authored stock, buying accepted
    items from the player, stock/restocking/credit-pool/buyback/
    pricing rules, opening the custom trading screen through Dialogue.

The Banker is already substantially implemented (recovered estimate:
~70% complete, per prior design context). VERIFY AGAINST CODE before
changing architecture or names — document what exists, don't rebuild
it from this recovery.

2. UNIVERSAL CREDITS
================================================================================
RECOVERED — ADOPTED. Totality's universal currency is Credits, symbol
**Ȼ**. The earlier Copper/Silver/Gold auto-deposit model is SUPERSEDED
by this.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2a. Two forms of Credits
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHYSICAL CREDITS: exist as inventory items, spendable directly where
    supported, drop on death like ordinary inventory, useful as quest
    rewards/loot/tangible world currency, do NOT auto-deposit on
    pickup or use.

    Recovered physical Credit items: Credit Chip (small value), Credit
    Pile (medium value), Credit Card (large value), Encrypted Credit
    Card (special/secured variant, exact redemption/decryption
    behavior TBD). Exact denominations were NOT recovered — do not
    invent them silently.

  ACCOUNT CREDITS: stored in the player's bank account, do NOT drop on
    death, more convenient for trading/transfers/remote services,
    require an account opened through the Banker.

  A trading screen must NOT be hard-gated behind having an account — a
  new player can still participate using physical Credits or whatever
  current implementation permits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2b. Dimensional currencies
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Future dimensions can drop their own physical currencies (Septims,
  Ryō, Zeni, Dollars, etc.) — distinct items, environmental
  storytelling. A Banker or Value Exchanger converts them into Ȼ. The
  base account stores universal Credits only; the Dimensional account
  tier (Section 3) can hold multiple currency types directly.

4. ATM
================================================================================
RECOVERED — ADOPTED. Non-craftable block entity found in banks and
settlements.

  Functions: Deposit, Withdraw, Transfer, view transaction history.

  Breaking/attacking an ATM: alerts guards, applies/advances a
  criminal flag, does NOT simply drop a usable ATM block.

  Interface intentionally simpler than the full Banker dialogue:
  current balance, four primary actions, transaction feedback, clear
  failure reasons (account restrictions, caps, insufficient funds).

  The ATM does NOT exchange dimensional currencies — that requires the
  Banker or another dedicated exchanger.

6. TRANSACTIONS — ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH
================================================================================
RECONSTRUCTION — PROPOSED (architectural glue inferred to make the
recovered pieces coherent; needs Stefan's sign-off before being
treated as canonical, same as any other PROPOSED item in Totality's
design corpus).

Economy should use ONE server-authoritative transaction service rather
than letting the Banker, ATM, merchant screen, Phone Store, and
Banking App mutate balances independently.

  public enum TransactionType {
      DEPOSIT, WITHDRAWAL, PURCHASE, SALE, TRANSFER_SENT,
      TRANSFER_RECEIVED, EXCHANGE, DELIVERY_FEE, SUBSCRIPTION,
      INTEREST, REFUND, BUYBACK
  }

  public record CreditTransaction(
          UUID transactionId, long timestamp, TransactionType type,
          long amount, long balanceAfter,
          Optional<UUID> counterpartyPlayer,
          Optional<Identifier> counterpartyNpcOrShop,
          Optional<Identifier> relatedItem, Optional<Text> note
  ) {}

  REQUIRED BEHAVIOR: all operations validate before mutation; multi-
  part actions are transactional; a failed purchase cannot remove
  Credits without delivering the item; a failed deposit cannot
  consume physical Credits; a transfer completes on both accounts or
  neither; every accepted transaction gets one immutable ledger entry;
  replaying the same network request must not duplicate a transaction.

  The exact Java shape above is PROPOSED, not recovered canon — reuse
  existing classes where possible rather than assuming this exact
  shape must be built from scratch.

7. ITEM VALUES AND MERCHANT DEFINITIONS
================================================================================
RECOVERED — ADOPTED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7a. Base item value
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Totality items receive an `ItemValue` at registration or equivalent
  static data. Vanilla items use a separate data mapping. Value
  belongs to the item/economy data, not separately authored per shop.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7b. Default sell pricing
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A merchant normally BUYS at 50% of base value. Individual shops,
  relationships, skills, reputation, item condition, scarcity, and
  special rules may modify this later. Product Condition (Food
  Ecosystem doc Section 6a) can affect food/fish/other condition-
  bearing products' price.

  Fishing already anticipates two distinct markets (Food Ecosystem doc
  Section 11l): culinary market (freshness, grade, traits, yield) and
  trophy/collector market (rarity, size percentile, coloration,
  location, event status) — this Economy document's pricing rules
  should be consistent with that existing split, not reinvent a
  separate pricing model for fish specifically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7c. Shop definition (immutable data) vs. runtime state
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Shop definition fields (recovered): shop_id, explicit sells list,
  accepts_tags (what the merchant buys), credit-pool limit, restock
  interval/days, stock limits. Any NPC can reference a shop_id from
  Dialogue data and open the same trading system.

  Runtime state (separate from the immutable definition): current
  stock, next restock time, current merchant Credit pool, buyback
  entries, any investment increase.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7d. IMPORTANT CORRECTION — merchant Credit pool direction
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An older note said "selling to a merchant increases their pool" — this
is mathematically backwards (a merchant paying the player OUT should
REDUCE what it has available, not increase it). CORRECTED, locked
accounting:

  - Player BUYS from merchant -> merchant Credit pool INCREASES (the
    merchant just received the player's payment).
  - Player SELLS to merchant -> merchant Credit pool DECREASES (the
    merchant just paid the player out of its pool).
  - The transaction is REJECTED or quantity-limited when the merchant
    cannot pay (insufficient pool to buy what the player's selling).
  - Investment raises the merchant's maximum or replenished pool (a
    Speech mastery or strong relationship can permanently increase a
    merchant's available Credit pool — exact progression/amount TBD).

  Do NOT implement the older, backwards sentence literally.

8. CUSTOM TRADING SCREEN
================================================================================
RECOVERED — ADOPTED.

  LAYOUT: left side 3x4 item grid, item name above each entry, price
  beneath in Ȼ. Right side: large item icon, name, description,
  statistics, lore. Top right: player Credit balance. Bottom left:
  BUY / SELL / BUYBACK tabs. Bottom right: confirm/cancel controls.

  BEHAVIOR: SELL only shows inventory items matching the shop's
  accepted tags/restrictions. Quantity uses a slider plus text input.
  Price updates live. Quantity capped by stock, inventory, player
  funds, AND merchant Credit pool. High-value operations get an
  additional confirmation. The Buyback tab is PERSISTENT rather than
  clearing at each restock.

  BUYBACK PRICE: recovered wording was "full buy price," which is
  ambiguous. CLARIFIED as two separate decisions rather than one:

    LOCKED: when an item enters Buyback, calculate and store a fixed
    `buybackPrice` on the buyback entry. That recorded price NEVER
    CHANGES afterward — market changes cannot alter an already-created
    buyback obligation.

    TBD: the FORMULA used to calculate the initial `buybackPrice` —
    whether it's exactly what the merchant paid the player, the
    merchant's normal retail price, or another rule — is NOT decided.

  TBD (remaining): global vs. account-tier buyback retention; buyback
  capacity and pruning.

10. ECONOMY ONBOARDING
================================================================================
RECOVERED — ADOPTED. Do NOT redesign phone acquisition — the existing

  The phone quest should: use one Basic Phone item, initialize it

  player encounter banking naturally afterward.

  The Banker's first quest should teach: opening an account, why

  The Banker quest and the Provisioner's "First Sale" are SEPARATE

  buying/selling items. Don't merge them into one mega-tutorial.

12. ACCEPTANCE CHECKS
================================================================================
  - Physical Credits drop on death; account Credits do not.
  - Depositing physical Credits cannot duplicate them.
  - A failed purchase neither removes money nor creates the item.
  - A merchant cannot pay more than its available pool.
  - Buying from a merchant increases its pool; selling to it decreases
    the pool (Section 7d — this is the corrected direction).
  - Buyback preserves the recorded transaction price, not a
    recalculated one.
  - Breaking an ATM does not drop a usable ATM and triggers security.
  - The Banker never acts as a "buy Credits" vendor.
