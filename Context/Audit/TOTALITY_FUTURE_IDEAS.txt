TOTALITY — FUTURE IDEAS (LIGHTWEIGHT TRACKING, NOT DESIGN DOCS)

STATUS: this is NOT a design specification. It's a short list of ideas
discussed but deliberately NOT designed yet — just enough context so
they aren't lost, and so a design-review partner (ChatGPT, or anyone
else looking at the full Totality document set) has visibility into
what's planned without needing full contracts/records/ownership
boundaries written out. When any of these actually gets designed, it
should get its own proper document (or a section in an existing one),
built with the same rigor as everything else — this file should
shrink over time as ideas graduate out of it, not grow into a design
doc itself.

================================================================================
1. FOUNDATION / ANOMALY SYSTEM (higher priority — before Arena)
================================================================================
A combined, Totality-original system inspired by SCP Foundation and
NTE (Neverness to Everness)-style anomaly games — NOT a literal crossover
or reskin. Stefan's own containment organization, with its own
branding, but deliberately keeping the load-bearing genre conventions
that make this kind of content work:

  - SCP-style CONTAINMENT CLASS naming conventions (kept, not
    reinvented from scratch)
  - NTE-style STAR-BASED anomaly difficulty rating (kept)
  - Actual, specific SCP-like/anomaly CONTENT — real authored
    anomalies, not just the mechanical shell

  ANOMALY ZONES are the most interesting part, and are NOT only the
  "door appears" mechanic. Confirmed via research on NTE's actual
  design (specifically the "Deep Blue Sorrow" anomaly / Sea Prisoner
  whale boss): the encounter can build up ENVIRONMENTALLY while the
  player is already traveling, before any portal even appears — e.g.
  an underground/underwater road that still has windows, so the
  player can see a Whale-like anomaly out in the water while still
  just driving/walking normally. Continuing along that same road is
  what eventually triggers a portal/gate to open, leading into the
  anomaly's own realm. So the full pattern has (at least) three
  distinct beats, not just one:
    1. ENVIRONMENTAL FORESHADOWING — the player is already in a normal
       space (a road, tunnel, corridor) that has some sight-line
       (windows, gaps, whatever fits the specific anomaly) letting
       them glimpse the anomaly before anything supernatural has
       technically happened yet.
    2. THE PORTAL/GATE OPENING — continuing forward is what triggers
       the transition into the anomaly's realm (this is the part the
       see-through-portal mod approach, noted above, would handle).
    3. A CUTSCENE-STYLE TRANSITION into the anomaly's dimension/arena
       — NTE uses an actual scripted story cutscene between "portal
       opens" and "you're now in the boss arena," not an instant cut.

  WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE THE ANOMALY ZONE ALSO VARIES — NOT EVERY
  ENCOUNTER IS COMBAT. Some anomaly zones should resolve through
  PUZZLES instead of (or alongside) a fight — matches how NTE itself
  mixes boss-fight anomalies with puzzle/exploration/story-focused
  ones rather than making every anomaly a combat encounter. This
  affects the eventual design: an anomaly's resolution TYPE (combat,
  puzzle, or some hybrid/other) should be an authored property of that
  specific anomaly, not a fixed assumption baked into the whole system.

  IMPLEMENTATION CAUTION #2 (in addition to the portal-rendering one
  above): it's not yet established whether CUTSCENE-STYLE ENCOUNTERS
  (scripted camera movement, timed events, a controlled sequence the
  player mostly watches rather than directly controls) are practically
  achievable in Minecraft's engine/modding constraints. This needs its
  own feasibility check, separate from the portal-rendering question —
  camera control mods/approaches exist in the broader modding
  ecosystem, but whether something cutscene-quality is realistic here
  is unverified. Same caveat as the portal visual: don't assume it's
  easy, check first, and a simpler transition (fade to black, a
  vanilla-style teleport with minimal camera scripting) can serve as a
  placeholder if full cutscenes turn out to be impractical.

  IMPLEMENTATION CAUTION (portal rendering, unchanged): verify the see-
  through-portal rendering trick is actually FEASIBLE/reasonable to
  implement in the current engine before committing to it. Minecraft
  is expected to eventually move to Vulkan, which will likely bring
  significant rendering-pipeline changes — building a custom portal-
  rendering system now, only to have it broken or need a full rework
  once Vulkan lands, would be wasted effort. Worth seriously
  considering DELAYING this specific visual feature until after the
  Vulkan transition happens, rather than implementing it twice. This
  doesn't block designing/building the rest of the Foundation/Anomaly
  system (containment, Codex entries, anomaly content, etc.) — just
  the see-through-portal VISUAL specifically; a plain teleport/loading
  transition could serve as a placeholder in the meantime if the
  system gets built before Vulkan arrives.

  CODEX INTEGRATION: SCPs/anomalies should be their own Codex category
  (Food Ecosystem doc Section 4's Codex/Discovery system) — same
  discovery-level progression (rumored/observed/studied/mastered)
  already used everywhere else, just applied to anomaly entries
  instead of fish/crops/ingredients.

  PRIORITY REASONING: this can come BEFORE Arena/Colosseum (below)
  because it doesn't need the full RPG/Equipment/Economy stack to be
  meaningful — it leans mostly on systems already built or in progress
  (Codex, dimensional/portal tech, exploration), not on a mature
  combat/gear/betting economy.

  NOT YET DESIGNED: containment mechanics, how anomalies are
  "discovered" vs. actively hunted, what the player's actual
  relationship to the Foundation-equivalent organization is (member?
  independent? contractor?), specific anomaly list, how dangerous
  anomalies are handled if they escape/threaten a settlement, whether
  there's a faction rivalry angle (per Totality's general faction
  design philosophy elsewhere).

2. ARENA / COLOSSEUM (lower priority — after RPG side is more complete)
================================================================================
Inspired by Swords and Sandals (Stefan recently replayed the
Remastered version). Mostly PVE, with PVP also possible.

  Core loop (Swords and Sandals-style, not yet adapted to Totality
  specifics): build a fighter, climb tournament ranks, face an
  increasingly wild opponent roster, gear up between fights.

  What made the reference game work, worth remembering when this gets
  designed: tournament-BRACKET structure giving fights narrative
  stakes beyond just "another fight"; an opponent roster with real
  PERSONALITY/gimmicks rather than reskinned stat blocks; escalating
  ABSURDITY at higher tiers.

  Totality-specific angle: later tournament tiers could pull in
  genuinely strange DIMENSIONAL entities as opponents, leaning into
  the multiversal/dimensional flavor already present elsewhere in the
  design (Wish, Dragon Balls, the Disease/Spell capstone framing) — a
  gladiator arena that escalates from mundane fighters into something
  much weirder at the top tiers.

  OWNERSHIP PRINCIPLE (already agreed, worth preserving whenever this
  gets designed for real): Arena should NOT invent its own combat,
  equipment, or economy — it should be a CONTEXT that reads from
  existing RPG combat, Equipment, and Economy systems (betting/
  wagering items, arena-specific currency or reputation), same
  ownership-boundary pattern used everywhere else in Totality's design
  (source system owns the mechanic, the affected/contextual system
  just uses it).

  PRIORITY REASONING: explicitly waiting on RPG/Equipment/Economy
  being more mature, since a gladiator-fight loop with nothing to bet,
  no gear progression, and no combat depth wouldn't be interesting yet.

  NOT YET DESIGNED: literally anything beyond the above — no records,
  no contracts, no opponent list, no bracket structure specifics.
